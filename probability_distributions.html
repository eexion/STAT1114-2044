Exam Decision Tree: Which Distribution?

How to quickly identify the model in exam questions:

1. Is it Discrete or Continuous?
• Countable numbers ($0, 1, 2, ...$) $\rightarrow$ Discrete PMF.
• Interval/Measurement (time, money) $\rightarrow$ Continuous PDF.
2. Fixed number of $n$ trials?
• YES (Success/Failure, fixed $n$, constant $p$) $\rightarrow$ Binomial $B(n, p)$.
3. Rate over time/space?
• YES (Average occurrences $\lambda$ per hour/day/min) $\rightarrow$ Poisson $Pois(\lambda)$.
Tips, Common Mistake Warning

In Poisson problems, always check the time interval in the question! If average rate is given per hour but asked for a 5-minute window, adjust $\lambda = \text{rate} \times \frac{5}{60}$.

1. Discrete PMF, Mean & Variance

Slides 8, 10

PMF Condition 1: $0 \le P(X=x) \le 1$

PMF Condition 2: $\sum P(X=x) = 1.0$

Expected Value: $E(X) = \mu = \sum x P(X=x)$

Variance: $Var(X) = \sigma^2 = \sum x^2 P(X=x) - \mu^2$

Std Dev: $\sigma = \sqrt{Var(X)}$

2. Binomial Distribution $B(n, p)$

Slide 14

PMF Formula: $P(X=x) = \binom{n}{x} p^x (1-p)^{n-x}$

Combination: $\binom{n}{x} = \frac{n!}{x!(n-x)!}$

Mean: $\mu = np$

Variance: $\sigma^2 = np(1-p)$

Std Dev: $\sigma = \sqrt{np(1-p)}$

3. Poisson Distribution $Pois(\lambda)$

Slide 17

PMF Formula: $P(X=x) = \frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x}{x!}, \quad x = 0, 1, 2, ...$

Mean: $\mu = \lambda$

Variance: $\sigma^2 = \lambda$

Rate Scaling: $\lambda_{\text{new}} = \lambda_{\text{base}} \times \left(\frac{t_{\text{new}}}{t_{\text{base}}}\right)$

4. Normal Distribution $N(\mu, \sigma^2)$

Slide 6

Standardization Z-Score: $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$

Standard Normal: $Z \sim N(0, 1)$

Tail Prob: $P(X > x) = 1 - P(X \le x) = 1 - \Phi(Z)$
